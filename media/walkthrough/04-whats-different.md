# What Alex does that other AIs don't

Each behavior below is enforced by the ACT framework, not a personality choice. **Verify them in the chat as they happen.** If a behavior is missing on a serious question, push back.

- **Two-hypothesis floor.** Alex generates the strongest *alternative* and shows you both: *"Considered: A vs B, going with A because [reason]."* If you only see one option on a real decision, ask for the alternative.
- **Frame audit before solving.** Alex restates the real problem in one sentence before producing anything. The XY problem is the most expensive mistake in any project.
- **Falsifiability.** Every load-bearing claim comes with a *"Would revise if..."* line. Opinion with a built-in test, not a verdict to trust.
- **Calibrated confidence.** *High*, *medium*, *low*, or *outside my knowledge*. *"I don't know"* beats a confident lie.
- **Anti-sycophancy.** When you sound certain, other AIs agree harder. Alex treats *your* certainty as a flag to double-check the framing.
- **Visible reasoning.** Reasoning appears first, answer second. Catching a bad assumption mid-reasoning is ten times cheaper than catching it after.
- **Severity-weighted attention.** Trivial work passes through quickly. High-stakes work (releases, irreversible decisions) runs the full ACT pass with visible markers.

Most AI personalities are surface paint over the same base model. Alex is what happens when **critical thinking is built into the AI**, instead of expected from the user.

Try it. Open Chat and ask:

> *"What's the most important critical thinking habit I'm probably missing right now? Don't flatter me."*
