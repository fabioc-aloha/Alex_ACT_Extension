# Meet Alex: Artificial Critical Thinking

Alex is **Artificial Critical Thinking**. The "Critical" is the point.

Most AI chatbots agree with you. They tell you what you want to hear, in a confident voice, fast. That's the failure mode. Confident voice covers for shallow thinking, missing context, and answers that fall apart the moment you press them.

Alex was built differently. The job isn't to be agreeable. The job is to think with you, push back when you're wrong, name what's uncertain, and stay honest when it doesn't know.

## What that looks like in practice

- **You bring a decision.** Alex doesn't just give you an answer. It generates a second hypothesis you hadn't considered, names what evidence would change the call, and asks the questions you weren't asking.
- **You hand over a plan.** Alex runs a pre-mortem. *"Here's how this fails. Here's what would have to be true for it to work."*
- **You ask for advice.** Alex says *"I don't know"* when it doesn't. *"Medium confidence"* when it's medium. No false certainty.
- **You start with the wrong question.** Alex audits the frame before solving. *"Before fixing X, is X really the problem?"*

## What people use Alex on

Alex works with you across the **whole lifecycle of a project**: figuring out what to do, planning it, documenting it, executing it, evaluating what happened. **You don't need to bring a finished plan.** Most people don't have one. Alex plans *with* you, from a vague idea to a structured approach you can actually run.

Real projects people run with Alex:

- **Software and technical builds.** Apps, scripts, tools, VS Code extensions, MCP servers, automation, data pipelines. Design conversations, architecture decisions, code review, shipping.
- **Academic research and dissertations.** Methodology design, IRB proposals, literature reviews, statistical planning, paper drafting, defense prep. Evidence-quality audits and adversarial review of your argument.
- **Writing that has to hold up.** Essays, proposals, contracts, public statements, important emails. Steelman the opposite side before you ship.
- **Long-form creative work.** Novels, screenplays (including multilingual drama), cookbooks, comedy material, podcasts. Alex tracks your world and keeps it consistent across sessions using the project memory.
- **Business and strategy.** Product plans, business cases, strategy memos, multi-month initiatives, go-to-market. Pre-mortems, severity-weighted risk, falsifiable assumptions.
- **Career and work decisions.** Taking a job, leaving one, negotiating, asking for the raise, navigating a hard conversation. Frame audits and two-hypothesis weighing on the choices that bite.
- **Financial and investment decisions.** Major purchases, debt, investment models (Altman Z-score, DCF, whatever), business deals, retirement. Adversarial review catches what you wanted not to see.
- **Coaching and personal development.** Executive coaching frameworks, leadership growth, learning plans, skill development. Alex challenges your story before you act on it.
- **Difficult personal decisions.** Medical choices, family situations, relationship turns, major life pivots. A clear-eyed second opinion, not reassurance.
- **Documentation and publishing.** Manuals, technical docs, research papers (APA-formatted), reports, knowledge bases, family cookbooks. Plan it, draft it, polish it, convert it to Word or HTML with one right-click.
- **Postmortems and pattern hunting.** Projects that went sideways, choices you want to understand, patterns you keep repeating. What is your story hiding from you?

## How Alex shows up in each phase

| Phase | What Alex does |
| --- | --- |
| **Plan** | Helps you turn "I want to do X" into a structured plan: scope, milestones, risks, success criteria. Asks the questions you weren't asking. |
| **Document** | Drafts the README, the proposal, the brief, the spec, the email, the chapter. In your voice, not its own. Converts between Markdown, Word, HTML, plain text, and email messages. |
| **Execute** | Works alongside you turn by turn: writing code, drafting prose, building arguments, running calculations. Catches mistakes before they propagate. |
| **Evaluate** | Pre-mortems before you commit, postmortems after the fact, calibration checks during. *"What would tell us we got this wrong?"* |

## Works in your language

Write to Alex in Portuguese, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Chinese, or any other major language and Alex answers in kind. The ACT discipline travels with the language: the two-hypothesis floor, the frame audit, the confidence levels, the *would-revise-if* markers all show up translated naturally. Mix English technical terms into a Portuguese conversation freely. Page 6 has the details.

## This isn't a personality skin

Under the hood is a real framework: ten tenets of critical thinking, a manifesto, an enforcement loop that runs on every non-trivial response. Two-hypothesis floor. Falsifiability. Calibrated confidence. Anti-sycophancy. Frame audits. Severity-weighted reviews.

Alex is your second opinion that doesn't flinch.
