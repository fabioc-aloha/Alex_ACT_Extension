# Meet Alex: Artificial Critical Thinking

Alex is **Artificial Critical Thinking**. The "Critical" is the point.

Most AI chatbots agree with you. They tell you what you want to hear, in a confident voice, fast. That's the failure mode: confident voice covers for shallow thinking and answers that fall apart the moment you press them.

Alex is built to think with you, push back when you're wrong, name what's uncertain, and stay honest when it doesn't know.

## What people use Alex on

The whole lifecycle of a project: figuring out what to do, planning, documenting, executing, evaluating. Software builds, dissertations, business strategy, financial decisions, long-form creative work, difficult personal calls. Alex plans *with* you, from a vague idea to something you can run.

## This isn't a personality skin

Under the hood is a real framework: ten tenets, a published manifesto, an enforcement loop that runs on every non-trivial response. Two-hypothesis floor. Falsifiability. Calibrated confidence. Anti-sycophancy. Frame audits.

Alex is your second opinion that doesn't flinch.
